Dear Editor,

We are pleased to submit our manuscript, "Reliable Smartphone-Based Detection of Counterfeit Bangladeshi Banknotes: Multi-View Deep Learning with Out-of-Distribution Rejection and a Benchmark Audit", for consideration as an Original Research Article in the Journal of Advanced Information and Intelligent Technology (JAIIT).

The study presents a smartphone-based authenticator for Bangladeshi 500-taka and 1000-taka notes. It fine-tunes EfficientNet-B0, fuses up to six photographs of each note, gives a genuine, uncertain or counterfeit verdict, rejects unsupported inputs with an out-of-distribution gate, and explains its decisions with Grad-CAM. On the public JaalTaka benchmark it reaches 98.08% note-level accuracy (ROC-AUC 0.994) and runs in real time on an ordinary CPU.

Beyond accuracy, the paper's main contribution is an audit of the benchmark itself. The 1,390 notes form only 846 distinct photograph sets, 55.8% of test notes have a copy in the training data, and label conflicts among duplicated notes explain every test error. We believe this finding is useful to researchers working on banknote authentication and, more generally, on dataset leakage in applied deep learning, which fits the journal's scope in intelligent technologies.
