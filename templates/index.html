How to photograph a note

  • Get close: the note should fill most of the photo.
  • Front side: portrait and the security thread.
  • Back side of the note.
  • Hold the note against light to show the watermark.
  • Avoid glare, blur and scanned images.
  • This is decision support. Confirm suspicious notes at a bank.
